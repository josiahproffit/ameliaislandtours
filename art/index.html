Shell art by Tim · Amelia Island native

Every piece starts on a morning walk down the beach.

Tim walks the Amelia Island shoreline the way other people read the paper. The cockles, oysters, whelks and driftwood he carries home become one-of-a-kind mirrors and wall pieces, set in paint and epoxy and finished by hand.

From the tideline to the wall

Nothing here is bought by the bag. Every shell was picked up by hand on Amelia Island, usually on the same walks Tim has taken for years. Some pieces hold hundreds of tiny coquinas. Others are built around a single find, like a horseshoe crab molt or a whelk the tide left whole.

When he isn't on the beach, Tim runs Amelia Island Tours, leading history and haunted walking tours through Fernandina. The same love of this island's stories shows up in the art: shipwrecks, sea turtles, and the faith that runs deep in this town.
